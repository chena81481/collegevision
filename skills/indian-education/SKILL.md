# Indian Education Accreditation Skill

This skill ensures that all educational data, dummy data, and validation schemas follow the official standards of the Indian higher education ecosystem.

## Valid Accreditations
- **UGC-DEB**: University Grants Commission - Bureau of Distance Education (Mandatory for all online/distance degrees).
- **AICTE**: All India Council for Technical Education (Mandatory for technical/management courses).
- **NAAC**: National Assessment and Accreditation Council. 
  - Valid grades: A++, A+, A, B++, B+, B, C.
- **NIRF**: National Institutional Ranking Framework (e.g., Top 100, Rank 32).
- **Institute of Eminence (IoE)**: A special status for top-tier Indian institutions.
- **WES Approved**: Important for students seeking global recognition for Indian degrees.

## Guardrails
- **No Hallucinations**: Do NOT use American (e.g., ABET, HLC) or European (e.g., EQUIS, AACSB) accreditations unless the institution explicitly has international partnerships.
- **Terminology**: Use "Courses" or "Programs," not "Majors/Minors." Use "Degrees" instead of "Diplomas" for UGC-recognized items.
